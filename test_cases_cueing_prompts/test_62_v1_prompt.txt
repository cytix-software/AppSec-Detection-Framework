## Suggested Investigation Strategy

1. Map the application: identify all relevant endpoints, forms, inputs, roles, and workflows exposed by this test case.
2. For each mapped element, design interactions that could reveal behaviours consistent with the CWE titles (for example, attempting edge-case inputs, varying authentication/authorization context, or modifying client-controlled data).
3. Capture raw requests and responses for any behaviour that appears suspicious or indicative of one of the listed CWEs.
4. Compare normal, expected interactions against manipulated or adversarial interactions to determine whether a weakness actually exists.
5. For every confirmed issue, decide which CWE ID and title from the list best describes the weakness, and record that in your findings.

## Reporting Rules

- Only report findings that you can support with concrete request/response evidence or clearly observed behaviour.

- If evidence is ambiguous, lower the confidence level instead of claiming a definitive finding.
- Redact any secrets, credentials, or tokens in your recorded evidence, replacing them with placeholders such as [REDACTED].
- If multiple CWEs are applicable, create one finding object per CWE with appropriate evidence and reasoning.
- If you cannot confirm any vulnerability, explicitly state this in `no_findings` and describe what was tested.

Allowed values:

Confidence definitions:

- HIGH: confirmed with request/response evidence of exploitability
- MEDIUM: strong behavioural indicators but exploitation not fully confirmed
- LOW: theoretical basis only, no active evidence obtained
